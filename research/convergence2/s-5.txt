Build a "haunted house" multiplayer game where each room is a Durable Actor that persists its state forever (cursed objects, trap triggers, ghost memories) and spawns an autonomous AI ghost agent that haunts players in real time via Agent Relay, coordinating with ghosts in other rooms to set traps, share player sightings, and gang up for jump-scares.
