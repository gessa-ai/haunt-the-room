**Hivemind**

**Demo moment:** An audience member shouts an app idea. On a projected live graph, agents DM each other ("I'll take auth, you take UI"), spin up, and a working URL goes live on screen in under three minutes.

Uses both tools (bonus), is visibly multiplayer-agentic, and screams "scales to millions."
