# Trailer R&D (L25, 14:48). Sources: knowledge of indie trailer practice (Derek Lieu's trailer talks, Steam/IGF trailers); no live search, time.

## 10 rules
1. Hook in 3 s: open on the most surprising true moment (the AI believing a lie), never a logo.
2. One-sentence premise by 0:15: "You are the ghosts. They are AI. Lie to them."
3. Gameplay is proof: every claim gets a frame of the real game within 2 s of being said.
4. Story = 3 beats max: setup (house, flyer, hunters), turn (phones pour in), payoff (the cabal falls for it).
5. Escalate rhythm: shots 3-4 s early, 1-1.5 s near the climax; cut on the music's beats.
6. Faces and voices sell it: punch in on whoever speaks; the hunters' own lines beat any narrator.
7. Silent autoplay: every beat readable with sound off; captions huge (>=56 px), max 2 lines.
8. Title late: hold the logo until after the hook (0:12), then a full name card at the end.
9. Contrast cuts: quiet still art -> loud live game; key art is breathing room, never more than 2.5 s each.
10. End card 4+ s: name, URL, one CTA ("Scan to join."), QR big enough to scan off a projector.
