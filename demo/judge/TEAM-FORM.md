# Builderbase: Create a Team

**Image:** demo/judge/cover.jpg (replaced by the new game cover from demo/cover/ when it lands, ~15:25)

**Team name:** Haunt the Room

**What problem will your team tackle? (<= 500 chars)**

AI characters in games are chatbots you talk at. We made AI something a whole room plays against. Everyone scans a QR and becomes a ghost hiding in a haunted mansion on the big screen. Four AI hunters argue at a table over Agent Relay and vote where to search, and you can lie to them: your lie sways their conviction and flips their vote. Every ghost is a Terse Durable Actor, so we can kill -9 the game mid-round and nobody moves.
